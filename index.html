Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

What gents grooming packages does Zierra Veon offer in Malabe?

Zierra Veon offers 3 signature men's grooming package deals: Haircut + Beard Trim + Wash (LKR 2,000), Haircut + Beard Trim + Wash + Styling (LKR 3,000), and the all-in-one Haircut + Beard Trim + Wash + 10-Minute Head Massage (LKR 3,000). Individual cuts, shaves, grey coverage, and oil treatments are also available.

Where is Zierra Veon Salon located in Malabe?

Zierra Veon is located at No. 757 / 28B, Kaduwela Road, Malabe, Sri Lanka. The salon is situated along the main Kaduwela-Malabe route, offering convenient parking and access for clients from Malabe, Battaramulla, Kaduwela, and surrounding suburbs.

What services does Zierra Veon offer?

Zierra Veon provides full-service salon treatments for men and women, including classic haircuts, beard shaping, balayage, hair coloring, keratin smoothing, relaxing treatments, manicures, pedicures, acrylic nails, and specialized facials using Kejo, Jovees, Ume Care, and Derma Pro brands.

What are the opening hours of Zierra Veon Salon?

The studio is open Tuesday through Friday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and Saturday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. The salon is closed on Mondays.

How can I book an appointment at Zierra Veon?

Appointments can be booked instantly by messaging the salon directly via WhatsApp at +94 74 318 1001 or by calling 074 31 81 001. Walk-ins are also welcome based on stylist availability.
