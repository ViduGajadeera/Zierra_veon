# Zierra Veon - SEO & AI Optimization Plan

To get Zierra Veon to rank on Google's first page and ensure AI engines (like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity) accurately describe your business, you need a combination of Local SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

AI engines don't just "guess" what your business is—they pull facts from structured data on your website and cross-reference it with high-authority platforms across the web.

Here is the exact, reliable plan to achieve both goals.

## Phase 1: Feeding Guaranteed Facts to AI & Google (Already Started)
The most guaranteed way to ensure an AI describes your business correctly is to hand it the data in a language it natively understands: Structured Data (JSON-LD).

*Action Taken:* I have proactively added a hidden JSON-LD script to the `<head>` of your `index.html` file. This code explicitly tells AI bots and Google crawlers:
- What you are: A `HairSalon`
- Your description: "Zierra Veon is a modern, full-service hair, nail, and beauty studio in Malabe, Sri Lanka..."
- Your exact location, hours, phone number, and social links.

When ChatGPT browses the web or Google crawls your site, it will read this code first and treat it as the "absolute truth" about your business.

## Phase 2: Optimizing for AI Engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.)
To make sure AI always has the right description when someone searches for "Zierra Veon", you must build consistency and context:

1. Create an FAQ Section on the Website: 
AI models love Questions & Answers. They frequently pull their answers directly from FAQ blocks. We should add a section to your site with questions like:
- "What services does Zierra Veon salon in Malabe offer?"
- "Who is Zierra Veon salon for?" (Answer: Men, women, and kids).

2. Maintain Strict N.A.P. Consistency: 
AI hallucinates (gets confused) when it finds conflicting data. Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number are typed exactly the same way on your Website, Facebook, Instagram, and Google Maps.

3. Get Listed on Directories: 
AI models cross-reference your website with trusted databases to verify facts. Make sure Zierra Veon is listed on local Sri Lankan business directories, beauty aggregators, and mapping services.

## Phase 3: Ranking on Google's First Page (Local SEO)
For a local salon, you don't just want the normal web results; you want to be in the Google Map Pack (the 3 map listings that appear at the very top of Google).

1. Google Business Profile (Crucial): 
If you haven't already, claim your "Google My Business" profile. Fill out every single field (Services, Hours, Description). This is the #1 factor for local ranking.

2. Review Generation: 
Google ranks businesses higher when they have a steady stream of 5-star reviews. AI engines also read these reviews to understand the sentiment of your business (e.g., if a review says "Best fade in Malabe", ChatGPT learns you do great fades).

3. On-Page SEO: 
Your website needs to mention the location naturally. Right now, your hero section says "BEAUTY THAT INSPIRES". We should slightly adjust the text across the site to naturally include keywords like "Premium Hair & Beauty Salon in Malabe".
